In time, a press is a hit; a press with no tile on the button, or a tile that slides past, counts against it. R-01's network is told where the tiles are as numbers; it never sees a picture, and this view is our drawing of its game. Each press is a whole-body lever press that takes the rat half a second to two seconds, so the tiles start slow and training speeds them up as it improves. Its hits, misses and off-tile presses make each hour's hit rate, which sizes that hour's $LABRAT buyback.

The sound is made in your browser: each note plays when the stream reports that R-01 hit a tile.

Rat buybacks

Simulated Test stream loading

One $LABRAT buyback an hour, sized by how well R-01 played that hour: hourly budget × hit rate. Its hits, misses and off-tile presses are counted through the hour (hit rate = hits ÷ all three); on the hour, the rate sets the buy and the rat clicks it through on pons. One buy an hour, instead of one per tile, keeps gas low. The budget comes from the coin's trading fees and is announced when it's ready; fixed caps per buy, per hour and per day apply. In the lever task, each clean press counts as a hit.

      How the rat buys back

      R-01 doesn't know what money is. It plays a game, and a fixed rule in the code turns how well it plays each hour into one $LABRAT buyback.

      1. It plays liveR-01 plays Rat Tiles while it trains: its head picks a lane, and a lever press hits that lane's red button as a tile reaches it. You can watch it above.
      2. Hits and misses are counted each hourA tile pressed in time is a hit. A tile that slides past, or a press with no tile on the button, counts against it.
      3. On the hour, the hit rate sets the buyHourly budget × that hour's hit rate, within fixed caps. One buy an hour instead of one per tile keeps gas low. The budget is announced when it's ready.
      4. The rat clicks the buy through on ponsThe rig opens the real $LABRAT page and lights one button at a time; the rat's head moves the cursor and its lever press clicks: the amount, Buy LABRAT, then Confirm.
      5. Checked, then sent from the buyback walletBefore anything is sent, the rig checks the transaction: the right coin, the right amount, nothing extra. Then it goes out from the buyback wallet above.

      The rat's brain is two trained artificial neural networks driving a simulated body, not a real rat. The same brain launched $LABRAT, and its fingerprint is on-chain.
